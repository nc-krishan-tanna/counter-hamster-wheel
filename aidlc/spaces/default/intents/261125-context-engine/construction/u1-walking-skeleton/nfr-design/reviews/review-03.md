### Summary

The row-level security design is sound in shape: no `BYPASSRLS`, a non-owner role, composite keys between knowledge tables, and a per-transaction setting. Three defects would stop a developer building it as written. The organisation hook would break health checks. The policy cast errors on reused pooled connections. The `service_accounts` lookup cannot give the specified `404` for another organisation's token. Each is a small fix.
