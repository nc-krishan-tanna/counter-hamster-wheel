### Summary

The pipeline matches the team's practices and keeps the self-hosted shape intact, but three gaps would stop the hosted deploy from working as described: the automatic rollback runs an old `migrate` against a newer schema, the Supabase role bootstrap and database connection path are not workable as written, and the wildcard certificate plan misses the bare service address and the DNS-challenge Caddy build.
