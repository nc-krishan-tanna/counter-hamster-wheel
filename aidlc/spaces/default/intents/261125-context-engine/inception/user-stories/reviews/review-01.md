### Summary

The story set is broad, well grouped and mostly traces to requirements with usable Given/When/Then criteria. It is not yet ready for engineering. The agreed approval rules conflict with FR3.3, visibility is assumed rather than defined, the Request changes path and multi-owner proposals are untested, two NFRs have no criteria, and nothing marks the walking-skeleton slice. These are fixable by edits and by recording the Q4 decisions as requirement changes.
