### Summary

The component structure is sound: Tenancy is correctly a separate root component, dependencies are symmetric and acyclic, and chat workspaces and connections are sensibly placed in IdentityAccess and Ingestion. The two points to weigh before approving are that the blanket row-level-security rule conflicts with the lookups and background jobs that must work across organisations, and that the operator support-access path (and its log guarantee) is not placed in any component.
