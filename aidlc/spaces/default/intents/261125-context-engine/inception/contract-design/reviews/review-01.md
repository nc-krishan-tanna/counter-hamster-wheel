### Summary

The mechanisms are sound and match Q1 to Q5. The gaps are in coverage of the worker and in-process boundaries: the worker units have no way to reach the data they need, and several in-`api` dependencies from components.md have no interface. The inline-assessment timeout and the missing dead-letter admin path also need a decision before the contracts can be built from. Four Majors make this NOT-READY.
