### Summary

The change is mostly coherent: the traceability ids match the document, roles, FR11.6, FR13 and FR14 line up, and the one-account-per-organisation, one-region and no-billing decisions are carried through consistently. It is not ready, because six tenant corners are missing or contradictory. Operator access conflicts with the raw-content privacy rules, operators sit outside the one-organisation account rule, and export and deletion are absent. Sign-up abuse limits, per-organisation binding of connectors and chat, and what suspension stops are also unstated. Each needs a requirement before design starts.
